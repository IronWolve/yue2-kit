        From a recording i

        Transcribe the melody of any song you have as a file, then render it with your own style and lyrics. The words are not transcribed, and the singer is not copied.

        From one of your takes

        Take the melody from a song you already made and render it somewhere else entirely — a ballad as a techno track, a German song in English. The chord symbols are dropped so the new style can rebuild the harmony, and the run switches to Melody only.

        Supply your own score ABC, for covers and edits

        Paste an ABC score to condition the song on a melody you already have. Available in full and melody modes; for covers, use melody mode with a score that carries no chord symbols.

        Sampling defaults are tuned
        Shortcuts that set the knobs below; the middle is the tuned default. Composition does nothing in Direct mode or with a supplied score.
